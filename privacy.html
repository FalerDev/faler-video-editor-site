Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Faler Video Editor ("Faler", "we", "our", or "the Application") handles information when users install or use the desktop application and its supported third-party integrations.

1. Information processed by the Application

Depending on the features a user chooses to use, Faler may process:

  • license keys, application version information, and a device identifier used for license validation;
  • configuration data and application preferences stored on the user's device;
  • video, image, audio, title, hashtag, schedule, and queue information selected by the user;
  • authentication information provided through authorized third-party OAuth connections, such as access or refresh tokens;
  • technical status information required to complete uploads, scheduling, connection checks, or error handling.

2. Local media processing

Video editing and rendering are designed to occur locally on the user's computer. Media is not uploaded to Faler solely for editing. When a user chooses to publish a video through a connected third-party platform, the selected content is transmitted to that platform as necessary to complete the user's requested action.

3. Licensing and backend services

Faler may communicate with its backend services to activate or validate a license, check application updates, and support connected-platform authorization flows. License-related records may include the license key status, a device identifier, activation information, and related technical metadata necessary to operate the licensing system.

4. Third-party account connections

When users connect services such as YouTube or TikTok, authorization is performed through the relevant provider's authentication process. Authentication credentials or tokens may be stored locally on the user's device and/or processed by Faler backend services only as necessary to provide the requested connection, upload, and scheduling features.

Use of third-party services is also governed by those services' own terms and privacy policies. Faler does not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

5. How information is used

Information is used only for purposes such as:

  • operating and validating the Application;
  • saving user-selected settings and workflow information;
  • connecting authorized third-party accounts;
  • uploading or scheduling content when requested by the user;
  • providing updates, diagnostics, and security-related functionality.

6. Sharing and sale of personal information

Faler does not sell personal information. Information is shared with third-party services only when needed to perform an action the user requests, such as authenticating an account or publishing content, or when required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

Local application data remains on the user's device until the user removes it, uninstalls the Application, or uses an available disconnect/reset function. Backend license records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the licensing system, prevent abuse, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

Third-party access may be revoked through the Application when supported and through the connected platform's account or security settings.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by the Application. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Faler is not intended to override the age requirements of any connected platform. Users must meet the minimum age and account eligibility requirements of the services they choose to connect.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Application changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.
